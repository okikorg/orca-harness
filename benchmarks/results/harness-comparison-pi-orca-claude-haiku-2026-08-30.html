Method and boundaries

Schedule

16 tasks × 3 harnesses × 1 repetition = 48 sequential attempts. Harness order rotated by task.

Isolation

Fresh fixture and session state per attempt; explicit file-tool allowlists; no shell, web, MCP, plugins, skills, subagents, or servers.

Interpretation

This is directional one-run evidence. OpenRouter's underlying endpoint was not pinned across all harnesses. Claude reported Amazon Bedrock; Orcacode and Pi reported only the OpenRouter gateway.
