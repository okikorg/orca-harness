Method and boundaries

Schedule

16 tasks × 1 harnesses × 3 repetitions = 48 sequential attempts. Harness order rotated by task.

Isolation

Fresh fixture and session state per attempt; explicit file-tool allowlists; no shell, web, MCP, plugins, skills, subagents, or servers.

Interpretation

This is directional evidence. OpenRouter's underlying endpoint was not pinned across all harnesses. Reported provider labels: Pi: openrouter.
